# Market Research Report

## Landscape Summary
The sports booking market in Pakistan is currently underserved, particularly in rural areas, with existing competitors failing to provide user-friendly booking systems and adequate coverage of sports facilities. Users express frustration with complex systems and limited access, indicating a strong demand for a more accessible and efficient solution.

## Pain Points & User Frustrations

### Lack of Sports Infrastructure (Signal: moderate, 4 sources)
Inadequate sports facilities and infrastructure hinder sports development and participation in Pakistan.
- 'The main reason of downfall of sports in Pakistan is lack of sports infrastructure.' [[7]](https://migrationletters.com/index.php/ml/article/download/11231/7523/27422)
- 'Sports Venues often have _members blocked slots_ thereby reducing open slots for booking to general public.' [[10]](https://medium.com/@prashantpansare/challenges-in-sports-venue-bookings-model-83b57113c538)

### Difficulty in Booking Sports Venues (Signal: moderate, 2 sources)
Complex online booking systems and limited availability of sports venues make it hard for people to book and play sports.
- 'Facility owners & staff have to get trained for weeks before being able to use it.' [[9]](https://www.runswiftapp.com/blog/5-reasons-why-your-sports-facility-needs-scheduling-online-booking-software)
- 'The _online booking system is complex_ and athletes and customers end up calling anyway.' [[10]](https://medium.com/@prashantpansare/challenges-in-sports-venue-bookings-model-83b57113c538)

### Limited Availability of Sports Venues (Signal: weak, 1 sources)
The number of sports venues is limited, making it difficult for people to find and book a venue to play sports.
- 'Above all, the _number of sports venues are limited_.' [[10]](https://medium.com/@prashantpansare/challenges-in-sports-venue-bookings-model-83b57113c538)

### Lack of Awareness and Importance of Sports (Signal: weak, 1 sources)
People underestimate the importance of sports and its impact on society, leading to a lack of investment and support for sports development.
- 'We underestimate the importance of sports and creativity and its impact on society now approach as a side hobby rather than something that completely shapes the way a person evolves' [[25]](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXOjiyTCCJs)
